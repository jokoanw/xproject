The Story in The Book

Chapter 1

Molly had been staring out of her window for about an hour now. On her desk, lying between the copies of Nature, New Scientist, and all the other scientific journals her work appeared in, was a well thumbed copy of On The Road.

She has spent the last ten years in this room, sitting under the poster with an Oscar Wilde quote proclaiming that work is the refuge of the people who have nothing better to do. Although many considered her pioneering work, unraveling the secret of the llama DNA to be an outstanding achievement.
